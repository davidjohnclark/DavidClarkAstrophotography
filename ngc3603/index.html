Nebulae · March 2020

NGC 3603 and NGC 3576

A challenging LRGB and hydrogen-alpha portrait of two southern nebulae.

Select the image to view it at full resolution.

LRGB data with narrowband hydrogen-alpha added to enhance the red channel, processed mostly in PixInsight. I was very happy with the result on a more challenging assignment.

The lower nebulosity surrounds the open cluster NGC 3603, a starburst region. The upper nebulosity surrounds NGC 3576.
