The Sombrero Galaxy

M104, a lenticular galaxy 31 million light-years away with a striking dust lane.

Select the image to view it at full resolution.

View the annotated image

The Sombrero Galaxy, M104, is a lenticular galaxy in Virgo approximately 31 million light-years away. It has a bright nucleus, an unusually large central bulge and a prominent dark dust lane across its inclined disc.

Its centre contains a supermassive black hole with roughly one billion times the mass of our Sun.
