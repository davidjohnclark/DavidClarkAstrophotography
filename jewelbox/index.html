The Jewel Box Cluster

My first astronomical photograph, showing one of the youngest known open clusters.

Select the image to view it at full resolution.

This was my first astronomical photograph.

The Jewel Box is one of the youngest known open clusters, with an estimated age of 14 million years. It has an integrated magnitude of 4.2, lies about 6,440 light-years from Earth, and contains just over 100 stars.
