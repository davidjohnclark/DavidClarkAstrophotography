Nebulae · 13 January 2020

The Orion Nebula

M42, one of the brightest nebulae and the closest massive star-forming region to Earth.

Select the image to view it at full resolution.

One of my very first astronomical photographs. This is the Orion Nebula.

The Orion Nebula—also known as Messier 42, M42, or NGC 1976—is a diffuse nebula south of Orion's Belt. It is one of the brightest nebulae and is visible to the naked eye.

M42 is approximately 1,344 light-years away, making it the closest region of massive star formation to Earth. The nebula is estimated to span 24 light-years and contain about 2,000 times the mass of the Sun.

It shines brightly in the red hydrogen-alpha spectral line, captured using a dedicated H-alpha filter.
