Nebulae · April 2020

Gum 15

A faint hydrogen-alpha emission nebula in Vela, rich with dust and active star formation.

Select the image to view it at full resolution.

Gum 15 is a faint hydrogen-alpha emission nebula in the constellation Vela, approximately 3,000 light-years from Earth. The region contains a tremendous amount of dust and many areas of active star formation.
